Shipping & Delivery

Free worldwide shipping, dispatched within 24 hours with full tracking.

Dispatch

Out the door within 24 hours

Orders confirmed before 2pm ET on a business day are typically dispatched the same day. Everything else goes out the next business day. You will receive a tracking number by email once your parcel is with the carrier.

Delivery times

United States2–5 business days
United Kingdom & EU4–7 business days
Rest of world7–14 business days

Times are estimates from dispatch and can be affected by customs processing.

Packaging

Sealed, protected, discreet

Vials are sealed, individually protected and shipped in plain outer packaging. Lyophilised peptides do not require cold-chain shipping for transit periods of up to two weeks.

Customs and duties

Documentation handled

For international shipments we prepare the customs documentation and manage clearance end to end. Depending on your country, import duties or taxes may still apply on delivery — these are the responsibility of the recipient.
