About Miro Biotech

High-purity research peptides, supplied with documentation you can verify.

What we do

A supplier built around batch documentation

Miro Biotech supplies research-grade peptides to individual researchers, university laboratories, contract research organisations and institutions in more than 60 countries. Our catalogue covers metabolic, recovery and repair, cognitive, anti-aging, blended research compounds and the accessories needed to work with them.

Every compound is released against a certificate of analysis, and the identity and purity data for each lot is published rather than held internally. If a batch does not meet specification it is not released.

Verification

How we verify purity

Each batch is analysed by an independent third-party laboratory. Testing covers identity confirmation by mass spectrometry, purity by HPLC, and appearance and solubility against the reference standard. The chromatogram is recorded on the certificate of analysis.

  • Identity confirmed by mass spectrometry
  • Purity measured by HPLC, typically 99% or higher
  • Batch-level documentation, not a generic certificate
  • Sealed, sterile-filled vials with batch and expiry labels
  • A digital COA available on request, before or after ordering

Ordering

How ordering works

We deliberately do not run an online checkout. Research procurement usually involves a quote, a purchase order or an institutional invoice, so we handle orders by email. Build a list on this site, send it to team@mirobiotech.org, and we reply with availability, freight and a total — usually within one business day.

Logistics

Shipping

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours with full tracking and insurance, free of charge worldwide. We prepare customs documentation on international shipments and pack everything as sealed vials in plain outer packaging.
