FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Shipping, documentation, storage and how ordering works.

Are your peptides for human consumption?

No. All products sold by Miro Biotech are strictly for in-vitro research and laboratory use only. They are not intended for human or animal consumption.

Do you provide Certificates of Analysis?

Yes. Every batch is independently tested by a third-party laboratory with a comprehensive Certificate of Analysis (COA) covering purity and identity. A digital certificate of analysis for any product is available on request.

What payment methods do you accept?

At checkout you can pay by Bitcoin to our published wallet address, or send the order by email and we invoice you. PayPal, CashApp, Zelle and card are available on request — tell us which you prefer and we send the details together with your invoice. No surcharges are applied to any payment method.

What is the purity of your research peptides?

All Miro Biotech peptides are verified at 99%+ purity through independent third-party testing. Purity data is published for every batch with full certificates of analysis.

Where can I buy research peptides online?

Miro Biotech ships research-grade peptides worldwide with free shipping on every order. Pay by Bitcoin at checkout, or email the order and pay by PayPal, CashApp, Zelle or card on request. All products are third-party tested with certificates of analysis. Orders ship the same day, within 24 hours, and typically arrive within 7-14 business days.

What is your return policy?

Miro Biotech offers a 30-day satisfaction guarantee on all orders. If you are not satisfied, contact our team for a full refund or replacement at no additional cost. See our refund policy page for eligibility details.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale pricing?

Yes. Volume discounts are applied automatically based on your order total, no code needed. Orders of $500 USD+ receive 5% off, scaling up to 20% off on orders of $5,000 USD+. Discount codes stack on top for additional savings. Visit our How to Order page for full details.

How should I store my peptides?

Store lyophilized (freeze-dried) peptides at -20°C for long-term storage or 2-8°C for short-term use. After reconstitution with bacteriostatic water, refrigerate at 2-8°C and use within 30 days. Never freeze reconstituted peptide solutions.

Do you accept cryptocurrency?

Yes — Bitcoin is a first-class payment option. Checkout shows the exact BTC address and a QR code, and you email us the transaction ID so we can match the payment to your order. No surcharge is applied to crypto payments, unlike many suppliers who add 5–20%. Orders paid in Bitcoin are processed the same as any other order.

How are research peptides shipped internationally?

Miro Biotech ships all orders free worldwide as lyophilized powder in sealed vials with plain outer packaging. Every order ships the same day, within 24 hours, fully insured and tracked from dispatch to delivery, and most orders arrive within 7-14 business days.

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes. Customs clearance and all documentation are fully handled by our team. Many orders ship from stock held domestically to you, so they arrive with no customs step at all, and when an order does cross a border we manage clearance from end to end. Orders to the United States, United Kingdom, European Union, and UAE will not require import duties at any time, whether shipped from local stock or internationally. For other countries, most orders also arrive with no duties; in the rare case local authorities apply an import duty or tax, that charge is payable by the recipient, and we cannot reship or refund a shipment returned over a refusal to pay it. Any shipment delayed, held, or rejected at customs for reasons other than unpaid duties is reshipped or fully refunded at no cost to you.
